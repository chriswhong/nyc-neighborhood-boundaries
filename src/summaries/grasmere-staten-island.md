Grasmere is a quiet residential neighborhood on the East Shore of Staten Island, bordered by Concord, Park Hill, and Rosebank to the north, and Arrochar and Old Town to the south and west. Named after the village of Grasmere in England's Lake District — a nod to the ponds and lakes that once defined the area — the name is attributed to Sir Roderick Cameron, a 19th-century shipping executive knighted by Queen Victoria. Brady's Pond, privately managed by the Cameron Club, remains a local landmark, with Brady's Pond Preserve serving as the neighborhood's only public park. The Staten Island Railway stops at Grasmere station, and Hylan Boulevard is served by the S78, S79 SBS, and several express buses to Manhattan.
